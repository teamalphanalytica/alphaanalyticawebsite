Telling Google and Meta which adverts worked

If you reach us by clicking a Google or Facebook/Instagram advert and then send a genuine enquiry, we tell that platform an enquiry happened so it can show our adverts to people more likely to be interested. We send the click reference and, for Meta, your email address and phone number in a scrambled (hashed) form that cannot be read back; Meta uses it only to match the enquiry to an advert. We never send the content of your message. You can object to this at any time by emailing us.

Cookies and similar technology

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. While you browse, your browser remembers which advert or link brought you here (in "session storage", which is cleared when you close the tab) so it can be sent with your enquiry. Our client portal uses a login cookie that is strictly necessary to keep you signed in.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We use these providers to run our service, under contracts that protect your information:

  • Netlify (website hosting, forms, login and database)
  • Twilio (call forwarding for client tracking numbers)
  • Resend (sending emails such as report notifications)
  • Google and Meta (only as described above)

Some of these providers process information in the United States. Where they do, transfers are protected by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or equivalent safeguards.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to work are deleted after 12 months. Client records are kept for the length of our contract and then for 6 years for legal and accounting reasons. Portal logins are removed when a client leaves.

Our clients' customers

When we run advertising and call tracking for a client, we handle the enquiries their customers send them on that client's behalf. In that case the client is responsible for their customers' information and their own privacy notice applies; we act as their processor.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to how we use it, or ask us to restrict it. Email us and we will reply within one month.
