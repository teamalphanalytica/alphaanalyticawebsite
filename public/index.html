Questions

Who owns my website and ad accounts?

You do. The domain, the ad accounts and the data are in your name. If you ever leave, we hand everything over.

How do you count an enquiry?

We track calls and form submissions that come from your website and ads, and every one is listed in your weekly report so you can see what you're being charged for.

How much should I spend on ads?

That's your call. We'll suggest a starting budget based on your area and what a customer is worth to you, and you can change it any time.

How soon will I see enquiries?

Most campaigns need a couple of weeks of data before we can say what's working. We'll tell you plainly how it's going, including when something isn't.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Local service businesses that win customers by phone or enquiry: gyms, trades, salons, clinics, restaurants and similar.

Is there a long contract?

No. One month's notice, any time.
