Questions

Who owns my website and ad accounts?

You do. The domain, the ad accounts and the data are in your name. If you ever leave, we hand everything over.

How much should I spend on ads?

That's your call. We'll suggest a starting budget on the call, based on your area and what a customer is worth to you, and you can change it any time.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Local service businesses that win customers by phone or enquiry: gyms, trades, salons, clinics, restaurants and similar.

How soon will I see enquiries?

Campaigns usually start bringing in enquiries within the first couple of weeks, then improve as we learn what works for you.

Is there a long contract?

No. One month's notice, any time.
