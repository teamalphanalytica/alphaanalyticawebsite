# The standard pitch

The same for every business. The proposal PDF supplies the specific facts; this is how to say it. (It is also in Admin, Prospects, "Your standard pitch".)

## The proposal in one minute

Page 1 opens with an **In short** box: what we found, what we'd do, what it costs. Everything after it is the evidence. A business owner can stop reading after that box and still know the offer.

1. **How your website is doing today:** six ticks and crosses. Green is working, red wasn't found, grey is "couldn't tell".
2. **Do customers find you?:** the searches a local customer would make, and whether the business appears.
3. **What's holding you back:** the three biggest things, in plain words.
4. **What your new website could look like:** a drawn concept for computer and phone, with numbered reasons.
5. **What we'd do for you:** four steps.
6. **What you get:** each benefit, why it matters, what it means for them. Then a worked example of what it could be worth (illustrative, labelled as such).
7. **Why choose us:** small and local, reviewed every week by AI, adverts that keep improving, they see what we see.
8. **What it costs, and what happens next:** pay per genuine enquiry, then three steps: a ten-minute call, we show the design, enquiries start arriving.

## 30 seconds, on the phone

"Hi, is that [name]? It's Jordan from Alpha Analytica. We're a small Manchester business that helps local firms get more enquiries from Google and Facebook. I had a quick look at your website and noticed [the main thing from the proposal]. We'd build you a new website that works well on phones and run the adverts, and you only pay for genuine enquiries, with no monthly retainer. Can I send you a one-page summary of what I found?"

## If they want to hear more (two minutes)

1. **What I noticed:** one specific thing from their proposal, said simply.
2. **What we do:** a new website, Google and Facebook adverts, and a private login showing every enquiry and call.
3. **Why us:** we're small, so you deal with me. Every Monday our AI reviews your results, and I check the changes and make them.
4. **How you pay:** per genuine enquiry. Spam and wrong numbers aren't charged. We agree the price on a short call.
5. **The ask:** "Can I send you the one-pager and ring you on [day] to talk it through?"

## Three questions to ask

- Where do most of your enquiries come from at the moment?
- How many a week would you like?
- What's a typical job worth to you? (Use their answer in the worked example.)

## What not to say

- Don't promise rankings, results or "being ahead of everyone". Say we review and improve every week.
- Don't mention past clients or results. There are none to show yet. Say you're starting out and offer to show the design first.
- Don't name or criticise their competitors.
- Don't email a sole trader or partnership cold. Call only, after the TPS check.
